What it tells us

The methods now run on the same larger datasets, and the 12-SNP cases are harder. Warm-start has higher observed quantum optimum rates than standard QAOA in this small study.

That warm start already uses a classical answer; its preparation work is recorded. This is not a quantum speedup comparison.

What needs attention

All six multi-angle fits reached the 60-evaluation limit. One 8-SNP warm-start fit did too. Three seeds and one shallow depth are not enough to choose a universal winner.

Small noisy increases are sampling observations, not proof that noise helps. Increase training thoughtfully before judging multi-angle.
