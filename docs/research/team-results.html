The exact setup

Pruning is selected, not automatically “better”

9 → 4 links

Baseline pairwise evidence, then Carla's maximum-spanning-forest rule. The graph stays connected.

On this toy, every pruned minimizer is still a full-model minimizer. That is checked, not guaranteed for other inputs. The native pruned optimum is 0; the full pairwise and MEC optima are both 1.

Small, fixed replay

Depth: p = 1. Sampling: 512 shots per quantum method per mode.

Training: local statevector COBYLA, one start, at most 60 evaluations. Warm start comes from a classical candidate, not a revealed optimum.

Noisy mode: FakeManilaV2 snapshot + Aer, compilation seed 7 and sampling seed 72. No readout mitigation, no live calibration, no QPU job.

Objective choices and training status

Alexandra's five scoring rules

Baseline pairwise, per-read weighting, per-site weighting, deduplication and full-read MEC.

On this toy: all five select the same two optimum labels, 01101 and 10010.

This does not rank them on harder data. The integration includes a separate counterexample where weighting changes the optimum. MEC is a common evaluation metric here, not the RZZ Hamiltonian.
